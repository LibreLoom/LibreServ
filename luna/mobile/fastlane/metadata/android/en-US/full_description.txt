Luna is a file box for your home that you own and control. This app uploads
the photos on your phone to your Luna automatically, in the background.

How it works:

1. On your Luna, open Settings → Security and create an access token.
2. In this app, paste the token. Or tap "Show as QR code" on your Luna and
   scan the code here. The app does not ask for your password.
3. Your photos upload to your Luna automatically. If an upload stops, the
   next upload skips the photos it already finished.

You choose when uploads happen (for example, only on Wi-Fi or only while
charging) and the folder on your Luna that gets them.

The app connects only to the Luna address you enter. It has no ads and no
analytics. You need a Luna to sign in.
