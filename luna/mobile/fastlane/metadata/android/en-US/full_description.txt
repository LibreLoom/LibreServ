Luna is a small home server you own and control. This app keeps a copy of
your phone's photos on it — automatically, in the background, over your own
network.

How it works:

1. On your Luna, open Settings → Security and create an access token.
2. In this app, paste the token — or tap "Show as QR code" on your Luna and
   scan it here. Your password is never stored on the phone.
3. New photos copy to your Luna automatically. If an upload is interrupted
   it resumes where it left off.

You choose when uploads happen — for example only on Wi-Fi, or only while
charging — and which folder on your Luna they land in.

This app talks only to your own Luna. There are no ads, no analytics, and no
third-party servers. It requires a Luna device (or a Luna server address) to
sign in.
